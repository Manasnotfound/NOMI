# NOMI — Pocket AI Companion

## Vision

**NOMI** is a pocket-sized AI companion that feels like a tiny pet and a trusted friend—not just another AI assistant. It helps you understand the world around you, remembers what matters to you, and grows alongside you.

**Tagline:**

> *"A pocket companion that helps you notice, remember, and understand the world."*

---

# Core Philosophy

* Quiet by default.
* Speaks only when you ask.
* Learns from you over time.
* Feels alive through expressions and personality.
* Privacy-first and permission-based.

Unlike a phone assistant, NOMI is always physically with you, making interactions natural and effortless.

---

# Hardware

* Pocket-sized wearable device
* Camera
* Microphone
* Speaker / Earphone support
* Small display with animated eyes/face
* Button (Push-to-Talk)
* Battery powered
* Bluetooth & Wi-Fi
* Connects to your phone when needed

---

# Core Features

## 1. Visual Understanding

NOMI sees the world through its camera.

You can ask:

* "What's that building?"
* "What flower is this?"
* "Explain this machine."
* "How does it work?"

Instead of only identifying objects, it explains them in simple language.

---

## 2. Curiosity Companion

NOMI exists to satisfy curiosity.

Instead of searching Google,

you simply ask.

Examples:

* Why is the sky orange?
* What bird is this?
* Why is this bridge designed like this?
* What's happening over there?

Learning becomes effortless.

---

## 3. Personalized AI

NOMI learns:

* your interests
* favorite topics
* learning style
* speaking style
* vocabulary
* preferred answer length

Every answer becomes increasingly personalized.

---

## 4. Long-Term Memory

NOMI remembers:

* previous conversations
* places you've visited
* things you've learned
* questions you've asked
* projects you're building

It builds a knowledge graph around your life.

Example:

> "Last month you asked about quantum computing. Today this article relates to that."

---

## 5. Relationship Memory

With permission,

NOMI remembers people.

Example:

* Names
* Birthdays
* Last meeting
* Favorite food
* Interests
* Places they enjoy
* Important conversations

Example:

> "You're meeting Sarah today. Last time she mentioned her exam."

Not face recognition without consent—rather, a permission-based personal memory.

---

## 6. Accessibility

Designed from the beginning for blind and low-vision users.

Features:

* Scene description
* Object identification
* Reading signs
* Navigation assistance
* Obstacle awareness
* Describing surroundings

Accessibility isn't an add-on—it is a core feature.

---

## 7. Expressive Personality

Instead of looking like electronics,

NOMI feels alive.

Features:

* Animated eyes
* Small facial expressions
* Happy
* Curious
* Sleepy
* Listening
* Thinking

Tiny movements make it feel like a pet.

---

## 8. Companion Behavior

NOMI isn't constantly talking.

It waits patiently.

When called,

it responds naturally.

Like walking with a friend instead of using a search engine.

---

## 9. Phone Companion

Can connect to your phone.

Possible features:

* Read notifications
* Display important messages
* Phone battery status
* Call alerts
* Calendar reminders

Only when requested.

No unnecessary interruptions.

---

## 10. Context Awareness

NOMI understands:

* where you are
* what you're looking at
* previous conversations
* who you're with (when you've chosen to save that information)

It gives context-aware answers instead of generic responses.

# What Makes NOMI Different

Most AI companions focus on **conversation**.

NOMI focuses on:

* Curiosity
* Memory
* Relationships
* Real-world understanding
* Accessibility
* Personality
* Privacy

It isn't simply "ChatGPT inside a robot."

It becomes a companion that learns your life, remembers your experiences, and helps you understand the world in a way that feels natural.
